# New England course expansion — Massachusetts, New Hampshire, and Vermont

Reviewed: 2026-08-22  
Staging batch: `data/import/new-england-expansion-north.reviewed.json`

## Publication policy

- A record enters the production candidate batch only when a current operator, municipality, resort, course organization, or public-agency page confirms that the course exists at the named facility.
- Directory records were used only to discover candidates and cross-check entrance or facility coordinates. They are not the published `source_url` and were not treated as proof of access, hours, fees, or same-day conditions.
- `OPERATOR_CONFIRMED_AVAILABLE` means the primary page currently presents the course as an available facility. It is not an “open right now” promise. Players should still check operator notices, weather, events, and seasonal restrictions before travel.
- Descriptions in the batch are new factual summaries. No operator prose, reviews, maps, or photographs were copied into FlightForge.

## Known uncertainties and follow-up

- Course and park status can change after review because of weather, maintenance, tournaments, capacity, hunting seasons, or private events. No record should be surfaced as a same-day guarantee without a live operator status.
- Dacey Field, Hawkins Woods, Burgess Park, Birch Park, Mount Sunapee, and The Quarries do not publish every access detail on one page. Their records deliberately direct players to check current notices instead of inferring a schedule or price.
- Mount Sunapee's resort mailing address is in Newbury while the course point sits on South Peak near the Goshen boundary. The batch uses the resort destination address and the course coordinate.
- The von Trapp course begins near the brewery; the batch uses the brewery-side facility address rather than the lodge's main entrance.
- Entrance points were cross-checked against current directory coordinates but should be replaced with operator-supplied GPS points when a course claims its FlightForge listing.
- All 26 records are scheduled for source review again by 2026-10-06.
